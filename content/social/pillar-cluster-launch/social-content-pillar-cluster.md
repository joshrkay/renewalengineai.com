## Article 2: Renewal Retention Math
Link: https://renewalengineai.com/resources/renewal-retention-math-for-p-and-c-agencies

### LinkedIn

Ask an agency owner their retention rate: "around 90%."

Ask what one lost policy costs them: silence.

Here's the math most agencies never run. At 88% retention, expected client lifetime is about 8.3 years. A personal-lines policy paying $216/year in commission isn't a $216 loss when it lapses — it's roughly $1,790 of lifetime value.

Scale it: a 2,000-policy book at 88% loses 240 policies a year. That's about $430,000 in lifetime value walking out annually. Every year.

The number that changes staff meetings: one retention point on that book is worth about $36,000 a year. That's the yardstick for every retention investment you'll ever consider — staffing, gifts, review cadences, automation.

We published the full walkthrough: both retention formulas (policy vs. revenue — if they diverge, you're losing your biggest accounts), the lost-policy cost model, the four ways agencies measure this wrong, and a worksheet a producer can finish by Monday lunch.

## Article 4: AI Automation vs. Hiring Another CSR
Link: https://renewalengineai.com/resources/ai-tools-vs-hiring-a-csr

### LinkedIn

"We need another person."

Every growing agency says it. Before you post the job, run this math:

A licensed CSR is $65,000-$80,000 fully loaded in year one — 4-8 weeks to recruit, 3-6 months to ramp, and turnover in service roles costs 50-200% of salary when it hits.

None of that makes hiring wrong. It makes hiring expensive — which matters enormously when the work drowning your team is renewal touches, lead response, and follow-up cadences. That's coverage work, and systems do coverage work better than people do: 100% of the book, every month, at 9 PM on a Saturday.

What systems can't do: angry phone calls, claims messes, coverage judgment, relationships. That's exactly what your best people are for.

So the question isn't "AI or humans." It's: for the specific tasks flooding your team, which tool is the right buy? We published the full walkthrough — loaded-cost tables, a year-one side-by-side ledger, the 4-question decision framework, and the two failure modes we see when agencies get the order wrong.

The pattern that works: automate for coverage, hire for judgment. In that order.
