## Article 2: Renewal Retention Math
Link: https://renewalengineai.com/resources/renewal-retention-math-for-p-and-c-agencies

### LinkedIn

Ask an agency owner their retention rate: "around 90%."

Ask what one lost policy costs them: silence.

Here's the math most agencies never run. At 88% retention, expected client lifetime is about 8.3 years. A personal-lines policy paying $216/year in commission isn't a $216 loss when it lapses — it's roughly $1,790 of lifetime value.

Scale it: a 2,000-policy book at 88% loses 240 policies a year. That's about $430,000 in lifetime value walking out annually. Every year.

The number that changes staff meetings: one retention point on that book is worth about $36,000 a year. That's the yardstick for every retention investment you'll ever consider — staffing, gifts, review cadences, automation.

We published the full walkthrough: both retention formulas (policy vs. revenue — if they diverge, you're losing your biggest accounts), the lost-policy cost model, the four ways agencies measure this wrong, and a worksheet a producer can finish by Monday lunch.

Link: https://renewalengineai.com/resources/ai-tools-vs-hiring-a-csr
